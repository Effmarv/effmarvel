TAVOLA.VIP: modern events, beautifully organised

Software for event planners. Design invitations and printed QR signs, send 3D invitation links, collect replies, seat every guest and run the day with your own teams. Weddings, birthdays, galas and conferences, all in one place.

The problem

Planning an event means juggling a guest spreadsheet, a printer, a seating chart on paper, a WhatsApp group with the caterer, and a hundred guests asking "where am I sitting?" at the door.

Planners were paying for separate tools for invitations, RSVPs and seating, then retyping the same guest list into each one, and printing signs that went out of date the moment someone changed tables.

Key decisions

One guest list drives everythingInvitations, replies, seating, place cards and the guest page all come from the same list, so a change in one place updates every printed QR sign and page.
QR signs instead of printed seating chartsGuests scan, type their name and see their table and tablemates. Late changes never mean reprinting.
Pay per event, not a subscriptionPlanners pay when an event goes live, in their own currency, across 9 currencies including GBP, EUR and NGN. Upgrading later only charges the difference.
Teams instead of one shared loginDecorators, caterers and venue staff each see only the parts they need. Allergy details only reach teams that handle the menu, and every change is logged.
Try everything before payingA free account and a public demo let planners build a whole event before they spend anything.

What I built

  • A design studio for invitations and signs, a Canva-style editor with templates, 13 fonts, foil and letterpress finishes, ornaments and a styled event QR code.
  • Print-ready files: posters, A4 signs, table tents, place cards with each guest's own QR, and stickers, with bleed and crop marks.
  • 3D invitation links in 20 designs that open like real paper, with smart replies covering plus-ones, meals, allergies and custom questions.
  • A QR event page where guests can find their seat and see the live programme, menu, map, Wi-Fi and photo album.
  • Per-sign scan tracking, so planners can see which signs get used.
  • Planner and team portals, plus an admin area and Stripe payments in 9 currencies.

Results so far

TAVOLA.VIP is live at tavola.vip, with a public demo where every screen works on sample data, so planners can try it before signing up.

Numbers to add: planners signed up, events created and signs scanned.

What I learned

The best feature was a tiny one: a QR code on a sign that says "find your table". It solved the most stressful moment of the day for guests and planners alike, and everything else was built around it.
