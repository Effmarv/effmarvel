How I build

Product judgement, modern tools

I don't start with technology. I start with the person on the other side: a braider mid-appointment, a seller answering the same price question fifty times a day, a promoter at a packed door.

I build with modern AI tools, which lets me go from idea to live product in days, not months. The speed comes from the tools; the judgement is mine: what to build, what to leave out, what it should cost, and how it gets paid for and launched.

1. Find a weekly painTalk to the people, study what they use today, and write down what it costs them.
2. Make the callsPricing, defaults and scope. For Rezzo: flat price, no commission, instant booking on by default.
3. Ship small, every dayReal releases to real users, often several a day, from first commit to app store.
4. Listen and cutWatch what people actually use. Keep it, fix it, or remove it.
