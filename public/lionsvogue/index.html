Case study · Nigeria

Lions Vogue: discover the party, skip the queue

An event ticketing platform and party marketplace for Nigerian nightlife. Party-goers find trending events and get QR tickets instantly. Organisers launch events fast, sell securely, scan at the door and get paid.

The problem

Nigeria's party scene moves fast, but tickets are often sold through DMs, bank transfers and paper lists. Buyers worry about fake tickets, organisers chase payments, and the door turns into a bottleneck.

Key decisions

Approved organisers onlyMerchants apply and get approved before they can sell, which keeps fake events off the platform and builds trust with buyers.
QR tickets, delivered instantlyEvery ticket arrives straight away with a QR code, so buyers have proof and the door can scan and validate in seconds.
Organisers stay in controlEach merchant manages their own inventory, pricing and refunds, and can see their payouts.
Discovery by cityEvents are filtered by state and party type, with a live "Nightlife Pulse" showing where demand is building.

What I built

  • An event marketplace with trending events across Lagos, Abuja, Port Harcourt, Uyo and beyond.
  • Instant QR tickets and shareable links for every purchase.
  • A merchant dashboard for inventory, pricing, refunds and payout visibility.
  • Door check-in: scan and validate tickets at the entrance.
  • Branded sales links for each event, so organisers can sell from their own socials.
  • Nightlife Pulse: a live demand heatmap across cities.

Results so far

Lions Vogue is live at lionsvogue.com and registered with the Corporate Affairs Commission.

Numbers to add: events listed, tickets sold and organisers on board.

What I learned

In a marketplace, trust is the product. Approving organisers and giving every buyer a scannable ticket did more for sales than any feature on the page.
